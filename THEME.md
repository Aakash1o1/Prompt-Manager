THis file has description for the new themes we are implementing


Light theme
Subtle Gradients: Instead of flat colors, consider very subtle linear gradients on elements like the main popup background or individual prompt cards. For instance, a background might go from #EAEAEA to #E0E0E0 vertically, giving a slight depth and metallic sheen.
Soft Shadows: Employ very soft, diffused box-shadows. Think of how light plays on brushed metal – subtle differences in elevation. For example, the main popup might have box-shadow: 0px 4px 10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08);.
Icon Treatment: The ☰ and ✏️ icons could have a slightly more "glossy" or embossed look with very subtle gradients or multiple shadows. The accent blue for active/hover states will make them pop.
Search Bar:
Background: #DDDDDD (Slightly darker cool grey than the main background) - This gives it a more pronounced, almost "machined" look.
Border: 1px solid #B0B0B0 (A slightly darker grey border for definition).
Placeholder: Secondary Text color.
Prompt List Items (Cards):
Background: #F8F8F8 (subtle off-white, as above).
On Hover: Change background to #E0E0E0 (a medium cool grey) and the accent blue comes in strongly on the edit icon.
Selected State: A very light tint of the accent blue, e.g., #E8F0F8, to indicate selection without losing the silver aesthetic.
Borders between items: Use 1px solid #D0D0D0.
Scrollbar: If customizing, a thin scrollbar with a light grey track and a slightly darker grey thumb would fit the aesthetic.





Dark theme
Sharp Contrast: The primary feature will be the stark contrast between the light text/accents and the very dark backgrounds.
Minimal Shadows: Shadows will be very subtle, almost imperceptible, as deep darkness naturally creates its own sense of depth. Where used, they will be rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4) or similar, blending into the dark.
"Pop" of Accent: The bright electric blue accent should appear to almost glow against the black, guiding the user's eye.
Search Bar:
Background: #2A2A2A (Darker grey than cards) - Provides a clear input field.
Border: 1px solid #404040 (Slightly darker grey border for definition).
Placeholder: Secondary Text color.
Text: Primary Text color.
Buttons (right of search):
Icons: Use a slightly desaturated version of the Accent Color when inactive, becoming the full vibrant blue on hover/focus. The background for these buttons could be a very dark, slightly transparent grey.
Toggle Switch: The track should be #[303030], and the active circle should be the full vibrant Accent Color.
Prompt List Items (Cards):
Background: #1A1A1A.
On Hover: Change background to #252525 (a slightly lighter dark grey) and the edit icon becomes the full vibrant Accent Color.
Selected State: A very subtle tint of the vibrant blue, e.g., rgba(0, 123, 255, 0.1) (a faint blue overlay) over the card background to clearly indicate selection.
Border-bottom: 1px solid #303030 for separation.
